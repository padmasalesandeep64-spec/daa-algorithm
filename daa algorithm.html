Algorithm Explorer

Smart Delivery Bag Packing — compare four algorithmic approaches to choose the most valuable parcels that fit within a delivery vehicle's weight limit.

1. Real-world problem

A delivery worker has a bag or vehicle with a fixed weight capacity. Each parcel has a weight and a delivery value/priority. The program chooses which whole parcels to carry so that total value is as high as possible without exceeding the capacity.

Why it matters: Delivery companies, warehouses and relief teams have limited space and need to prioritize what they transport.

What this program computes: The selected parcels, their total weight and total value, using Brute Force, Divide and Conquer, Dynamic Programming and Greedy methods. Greedy is a fast heuristic and may not give the optimal answer.

2. Enter your data

Use positive whole-number weights and non-negative values. Example: Laptop,5,60

How to use

  1. Enter the maximum carrying capacity.
  2. Enter each parcel's name, weight and value.
  3. Click Run Algorithms.
  4. Below, you will see a comparison, then pick an algorithm tab to see its explanation, step-by-step working on your data, source code and output.

For quick performance, use 20 or fewer parcels in this demo. Dynamic Programming uses a capacity-based table.

3. Results overview

All four algorithms ran on your data. Exact methods always agree; Greedy may differ.
